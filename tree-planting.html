Tree and hedge planting
The service

Planted in the right place, the right way

A tree that's planted well gets off to a far better start. We prepare the ground, plant at the right depth, stake where needed and finish around the base.

We plant single trees, rows of trees, and new hedging along boundaries and driveways.

What's included

  • Ground preparation
  • Planting trees and hedging
  • Staking and ties where needed
  • Finishing and tidying around the base
  • Clearing away all waste
  • Advice on aftercare and watering
Timing

Winter is planting season

Late autumn to early spring is the traditional planting season. Trees are dormant, so they can settle their roots in before the spring growth starts.

Book your planting this winter and your new trees and hedging will be in the ground and ready to grow when spring arrives.
