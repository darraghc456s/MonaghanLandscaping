Power washing
The service

Back to the original surface

Moss, algae and dirt build up over the year, and in winter they make patios and paths slippery. A proper power wash takes it all off and brings the colour of the surface back.

We clean patios, driveways, paths, steps and walls.

What's included

  • Patios and paving
  • Driveways
  • Paths and steps
  • Garden walls
  • Clearing moss and algae
  • Rinsing away all the dirt afterwards
Timing

Get it cleaned before Christmas

Wet weather and fallen leaves leave paths and patios green and slippery over the winter. A clean now makes them safer to walk on.

If you have people visiting over Christmas, a clean driveway and front path makes the whole house look better from the road.

Patios, paving, driveways, paths, steps and garden walls.

We adjust the pressure to the surface so it cleans without damaging the stone, slabs or joints.
