Reseeding and new lawns
The service

Ground prepared properly, then sown

A lawn only comes through as well as the ground under it. We clear and rotavate the area, level it, and sow new seed, whether that's patching a tired lawn or starting from bare soil.

For a new build or a garden that's been dug up, we can rotavate the whole area and lay out a new lawn from scratch.

What's included

  • Clearing weeds, moss and debris
  • Rotavating the ground
  • Levelling and raking
  • Sowing new lawn seed
  • Overseeding thin or patchy lawns
  • Advice on watering and first cut
Timing

Get it done now, enjoy it in spring

Getting the ground prepared and sown ahead of spring means the lawn is established by the time you want to use it.

Not sure if your lawn needs reseeding or a full new lawn? Describe it in the quote form, or send photos when we reply, and we'll tell you what it needs.

Questions

Common questions

Can you reseed just the bare patches?

Yes. Patchy lawns can be overseeded in the worn areas without starting again. If most of the lawn is moss or bare, a full rotavate and resow is usually the better option.

Do you do new lawns on new build sites?

Yes. We rotavate, level and sow new lawns on new builds and on gardens that have been dug up.

How long before I can use the lawn?

It depends on the weather and the time of year. We'll talk you through what to expect and when to give it a first cut.

Do you cover my area?

We cover Monaghan, Louth and Meath. Put your town in the quote form and we'll confirm.
